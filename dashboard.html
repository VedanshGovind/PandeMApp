How it works

From a puddle to prevention

🔍

Explore

See illness reports, breeding risks and animal-disease reports around your neighbourhood.

📝

Report

Log a confirmed or suspected case, a water problem that could breed mosquitoes, or a disease caught from an animal.

📍

Map

Clusters build a live risk picture so cleanup, fogging and vaccination can be targeted.

🌱

Improve

Fewer stagnant-water sites means fewer mosquitoes and fewer cases.

🔒 Privacy

Your Privacy Matters

Personal identities and exact home locations are not displayed publicly. Health-related reports are intended to be represented approximately or anonymously to help protect user privacy.

Community-reported information should not be considered an official medical diagnosis, medical advice, or government outbreak declaration.
