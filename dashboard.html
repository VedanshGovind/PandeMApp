Why volunteer

One minute from you can protect a whole street

PandeMApp only works because ordinary people tell it what they see. Official systems often hear about a problem late — your report can be the first signal for your neighbourhood.

📝 Make a report
  • ⏱️
    Earlier warningsA cluster of reports can show a problem days before it appears in formal figures.
  • 🎯
    Help the right fix reach the right placeClear maps help cleanup, fogging and vaccination teams go where they are needed first.
  • 💧
    Breeding sites are fixableA puddle, a blocked drain or a pile of garbage you flag today can be cleared before it breeds mosquitoes.
  • 🔒
    You stay privatePins are shifted by up to 150 m and no name or contact details are attached. No account is needed.
  • 🤝
    Neighbours look out for each otherReports help the people around you decide when to take extra care — and the next person to report builds on yours.

If an animal has bitten or scratched you, get medical care first. Rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms begin, but it is preventable: wash the wound with soap and running water for 15 minutes, then go to a hospital or clinic for post-exposure vaccination — and immunoglobulin if the wound is deep. Go today, not tomorrow. Filing this report does not replace that.
